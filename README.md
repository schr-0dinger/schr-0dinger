I'm a physician with a passion for engineering software that solves meaningful problems.

My interests span healthcare technology, artificial intelligence, full-stack development, developer tooling, Android internals, Linux systems, reverse engineering, and compiler design. I enjoy exploring how complex systems work - whether biological or computational - and applying that understanding to build reliable, elegant software.

I believe that innovation often happens where disciplines meet. My goal is to create technology that is technically sound, thoughtfully designed, and capable of making a real-world impact.
